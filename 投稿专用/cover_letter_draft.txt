Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Target-Preserving Detector Policy for Structured-Clutter Suppression at Low False-Alarm Rates" for consideration as a regular paper in IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems.

This paper addresses a practical but underemphasized issue in low-false-alarm radar detection: structured-clutter suppression can improve background cleanliness while simultaneously removing weak target support. To address this, we propose TP-SSCS, a target-preserving detector policy that combines a rank-matched residual branch with a compact raw-evidence rescue branch under an explicit false-alarm calibration rule.

The manuscript makes three main contributions:
1. It identifies and measures the conflict between clutter attenuation and target preservation on public AISTAP-SIM samples.
2. It introduces a frozen detector policy that preserves weak target evidence while maintaining calibrated low-false-alarm performance.
3. It validates the method on official AISTAP-SIM full-test assets and provides bounded external checks on IPIX and SSDD.

Across 210 target-bearing frames and seven operating points on the official AISTAP-SIM assets, TP-SSCS improves detection performance over both raw maps and rank-matched low-rank residual baselines under matched empirical false-alarm calibration. We believe the manuscript will be of interest to readers working on radar detection, structured-clutter suppression, and robust sensing under strict false-alarm constraints.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems.
