# AISTAP Reopen Conditions Card

Date: 2026-07-13

## Purpose

This card states exactly when the current final lock may be reopened for further evidence-class upgrades.

## Reopen only if at least one of these happens

1. A new data source becomes available that is materially beyond the current public AISTAP-SIM sample boundary.
2. A deployable target-preservation branch is actually built and shows a better frontier than the current low-rank baseline.
3. A finished detector result is obtained that changes the evidence class from scaffold-stage to deployable detector-stage.

## Do not reopen for

- Another summary page.
- Another packaging polish pass.
- Another boundary explanation.
- Another comparison ledger with no new evidence class.

## Why this matters

The current final lock already has dense operating-surface evidence, target-preservation diagnostics, trainability, stress tests, and a complete five-reference comparison stack. Without a new evidence class, additional work is likely to be low leverage.

## Operational use

If none of the three reopen conditions are true, keep the scope locked and treat the current comparison stack as the final state.
