# AISTAP Final Improvement Path Map

Date: 2026-07-13

## Purpose

This map converts the final comparison scorecard into a concrete improvement path against the five-reference target set. It does not reopen the current experimental scope. It records what would have to change, in principle, for the paper to move from "competitive on evidence structure" to "materially ahead on comparison position."

## Reference-linked lift map

### Closed submission / package references

What they still beat us on:

- final submission closure
- package-level finish
- no-drift packaging authority

What would move AISTAP closer:

- a fully finalized submission package with no remaining draft-boundary ambiguity
- a stronger end-to-end packaging discipline that reads as already closed rather than scaffold-bounded

### Claim-hygiene / validation-layering reference

What they still beat us on:

- mature validation layering across supported, bounded, and unsupported claims

What would move AISTAP closer:

- stronger direct mapping from each figure/table/result paragraph to the supported claim class
- fewer sentences that need boundary phrases to stay valid

### Reproducibility-first reference

What they still beat us on:

- full strong-baseline closure
- runnable/partial/proxy/blocked ledger as a mature system

What would move AISTAP closer:

- a deployable target-preservation branch
- a finished detector result on top of the current scaffold

### Scaling / protocol-sensitive references

What they still beat us on:

- clear stable-vs-dependent result characterization across multiple mature feeds or protocols

What would move AISTAP closer:

- a broader external dataset or protocol family that shows whether the current frontier remains stable
- a stronger generalization story than the current public-sample frontier

### Battery-style ranking / ensemble sensitivity reference

What they still beat us on:

- a mature ranking picture with average metrics, split wins, and ensemble sensitivity all fully integrated

What would move AISTAP closer:

- a richer frontier story that distinguishes diagnostic upper bounds, trainable-scaffold results, and any future deployable branch
- a clearer boundary between operating-policy evidence and any downstream ranking claim

## What this means now

The paper is already ahead on several evidence-layering dimensions, but the five-reference win condition would still require at least one evidence-class upgrade:

- new data source, or
- deployable target-preservation branch, or
- finished detector result.

Without one of those, the right move is to keep the current final lock and use this map only as the future lift checklist.
