## Overall verdict

The current paper does not yet beat all five reference packages in the unconditional sense required for a final victory claim.

What it does have now is:

- stronger evidence layering,
- stronger operating-policy detail,
- stronger trainability evidence,
- and stronger boundary discipline than the early versions of the manuscript.

What it still lacks for a true win:

- a deployable target-preservation branch,
- a finished detector result,
- a new data source,
- and a claim boundary that could honestly support an unconditional cross-reference victory statement.
